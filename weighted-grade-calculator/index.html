Weighted Grade Summary

A weighted grade multiplies each category average by its assigned percentage weight. Summing these weighted products yields your exact overall course standing.

Weighted Grade = [ (Score₁ × Weight₁) + (Score₂ × Weight₂) + ... ] ÷ Total Weight

Understanding Weighted Grading Systems

In high school and college courses, teachers frequently assign different percentage weights to distinct categories of work. This ensures that major exams carry more weight than daily homework exercises.

Mathematical Formula:
Weighted Grade = [ (Score1 × Weight1) + (Score2 × Weight2) + ... + (Scoren × Weightn) ] ÷ (Total Weight)

How Weight Normalization Works Mid-Semester

If you are checking your grade in the middle of a semester and have only completed Homework (20%) and Midterm (30%) for a total of 50% completed weight:

Normalized Grade = (19.0% + 25.8%) ÷ (0.50) = 44.8 ÷ 0.50 = 89.60%

Our tool automatically handles this normalization so your current grade always reflects your true classroom standing.

Frequently Asked Questions

To calculate a weighted grade: 1. Multiply each category score (in percent) by its assigned weight percentage. 2. Add all the resulting products together. 3. Divide by the total weight (if weights sum to 100%, simply take the sum). Example: Homework (90% score × 20% weight = 18), Quizzes (80% × 20% = 16), Exams (85% × 60% = 51). Overall Grade = 18 + 16 + 51 = 85.0%.
