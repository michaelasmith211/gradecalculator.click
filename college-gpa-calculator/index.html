College GPA Quick Reference

College GPA is calculated by dividing total quality points (Credit Hours × Letter Grade Points) by total attempted credit hours.

College GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Attempted Credit Hours

How University Quality Points Are Determined

In higher education, your Grade Point Average is not a simple average of your letter grades. Because different college courses carry different workloads (expressed in Credit Hours or Semester Hours), every course generates Quality Points:

Quality Point Formula:
Quality Points = Course Credit Hours × Numerical Grade Value (e.g. 4.0 credits × 3.7 [A-] = 14.8 Quality Points)

Latin Honors & Dean's List Thresholds

Colleges award academic honors based on cumulative GPA upon graduation:

Cum Laude3.50 – 3.69 GPA

"With Praise" — Top 15–20% of graduating class.

Magna Cum Laude3.70 – 3.89 GPA

"With Great Praise" — Top 5–10% of graduating class.

Summa Cum Laude3.90 – 4.00 GPA

"With Highest Praise" — Top 1–3% of graduating class.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quality points represent the numerical weight of your academic work. For every course, multiply the course credit hours (e.g. 3.0 credits) by the letter grade point value (e.g. A = 4.0). A 3-credit course with an 'A' yields 12.0 quality points.
