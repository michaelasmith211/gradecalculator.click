Final Grade Calculator Quick Guide

To find what score you need on a final exam, subtract the weighted contribution of your current grade from your desired final grade, then divide by the decimal weight of the final exam.

Required Score = [ Desired Grade - (Current Grade × (1 - Weight)) ] ÷ Weight

The Final Exam Grade Formula Explained

The math behind final exam calculations is based on a standard two-part weighted average. Your final overall course grade consists of two components:

1. Prior Coursework Contribution

Current Grade × (1 - Final Exam Weight)

2. Final Exam Contribution

Final Exam Score × Final Exam Weight

Formula:
Required Final Exam Score = [ Desired Grade - (Current Grade × (1 - Final Exam Weight)) ] ÷ Final Exam Weight

Practical Real-World Example

Let's walk through an example where Sarah wants to finish her Chemistry class with an A- (90%):

  • Current Grade: 87%
  • Desired Overall Grade: 90%
  • Final Exam Weight: 25% (0.25 as a decimal)
Step 1: Calculate prior weight = 1 - 0.25 = 0.75
Step 2: Calculate prior contribution = 87 × 0.75 = 65.25%
Step 3: Subtract from target = 90 - 65.25 = 24.75% needed from exam
Step 4: Divide by exam weight = 24.75 ÷ 0.25 = 99.0%

Sarah needs a 99.0% on her final exam to reach an overall 90% in the class.

Strategic Tips for Final Exam Preparation

  • Identify high-yield study topics: Review past midterms and quizzes since final exams are typically cumulative.
  • Calculate multiple scenarios: Know both your stretch target (e.g. A-) and your safety floor (e.g. maintaining a solid B).
  • Check for syllabus curve policies: Some professors replace a lower midterm score with a higher final exam grade.
