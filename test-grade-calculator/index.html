Test Grade Quick Reference

Test score percentage is calculated by subtracting incorrect answers from total questions, dividing by total questions, and multiplying by 100.

Test Score (%) = [ (Total Questions - Wrong Answers) ÷ Total Questions ] × 100

How to Use the Easy Grader Score Chart

Traditional cardboard "EZ Graders" have been used by educators for decades to speed up test evaluation. Our digital version generates a custom grading matrix for any test size (from 5 to 200 questions) in real time:

  1. Enter your test's total question count in the Total Questions field above.
  2. Scroll through the Easy Grader Quick Reference table to find the number of incorrect marks.
  3. Read off the exact percentage and letter grade immediately without recalculating each paper.

Missed Questions Cutoff Guide for Popular Test Sizes

25-Question Test
  • • A (90%+): Miss 0 to 2
  • • B (80%+): Miss 3 to 5
  • • C (70%+): Miss 6 to 7
  • • D (60%+): Miss 8 to 10
50-Question Test
  • • A (90%+): Miss 0 to 5
  • • B (80%+): Miss 6 to 10
  • • C (70%+): Miss 11 to 15
  • • D (60%+): Miss 16 to 20
100-Question Test
  • • A (90%+): Miss 0 to 10
  • • B (80%+): Miss 11 to 20
  • • C (70%+): Miss 21 to 30
  • • D (60%+): Miss 31 to 40
