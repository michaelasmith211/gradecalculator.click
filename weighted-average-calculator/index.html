Weighted Average Key Facts

To calculate a weighted average, multiply each value by its corresponding weight, sum the resulting products, and divide by the sum of the weights.

Weighted Average = [ (Score₁ × Weight₁) + (Score₂ × Weight₂) + ... ] ÷ Total Weight

Understanding the Weighted Average Formula

In statistics and academic grading, an unweighted average gives identical importance to every item. In contrast, a weighted average assigns different levels of importance based on syllabus weightings or dataset significance.

Mathematical Formula:
Weighted Average = ∑ (wi × xi) ÷ ∑ wi

Worked Academic Example

Consider a college economics syllabus with the following weighted grading categories:

  • Homework (20% Weight): Score = 95% → Contribution = 0.20 × 95 = 19.0%
  • Quizzes (15% Weight): Score = 84% → Contribution = 0.15 × 84 = 12.6%
  • Midterm Exam (25% Weight): Score = 88% → Contribution = 0.25 × 88 = 22.0%
  • Final Exam (40% Weight): Score = 91% → Contribution = 0.40 × 91 = 36.4%
Sum of Contributions: 19.0 + 12.6 + 22.0 + 36.4 = 90.0%
Letter Grade: A- (3.7 GPA Quality Points).
